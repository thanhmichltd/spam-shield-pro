FAQ
Does Spam Shield Pro require an account?

No. There's no sign-up or login - every feature is unlocked from the first launch.

Why does the app need to be my default phone app?

Android only runs call-screening and call-blocking for whichever app is set as the default phone (dialer) app. Setting Spam Shield Pro as default is what makes spam-call blocking actually work.

Does Number Tracker send my lookups to a server?

No. Caller ID and spam-risk lookups are calculated entirely on your device, against a database bundled with the app and a local cache. Your lookups aren't sent anywhere for this purpose.

What does the Unseen tab actually read?

It uses Android's notification access to show previews of messages you haven't opened yet, so you can check them inside Spam Shield Pro without unlocking each messaging app separately.

Is Spam Shield Pro free?

Yes - it's free to download and every feature is unlocked from the first launch, with no subscription required.
