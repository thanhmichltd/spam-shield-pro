Privacy Policy

Spam Shield Pro · Thanh Mich LTD

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Spam Shield Pro ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and operated by Thanh Mich LTD ("Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, what we actually collect and transmit off your device, and what stays on your device. We wrote it to match what the App's code does — not a generic template.

If you have questions, contact us at thanhmichltd@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • Spam Shield Pro does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • Caller-ID, spam-risk scoring, and number lookup are performed entirely on your device, against a database bundled with the App and a local cache. Your contacts, call history, and the numbers you look up are not sent to our servers or to any third party for this purpose.
  • The App uses third-party advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting SDKs (listed in Section 4), which do collect device/advertising identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • The App has no in-app purchases; a billing library is bundled only to support an internal "ad-free" flag and is not used for any purchase flow.

Call Screening, Default Phone App, and In-Call Screen

Spam Shield Pro can register as a call-screening app and can be set as your device's default phone (dialer) app. When enabled, the App evaluates incoming numbers against its local spam database and your settings to decide whether to allow, flag, or silently block a call — entirely on your device. The App also shows its own in-call screen so you can see caller-ID and spam-risk information while a call is active.

You can revoke the default-dialer role or disable call screening at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Default apps, or from the App's own settings.

Notification Access ("Unseen" feature) — Prominent Disclosure

The App includes an optional feature ("Unseen") that uses Android's Notification Listener API to let you review notification previews (e.g., message previews from your messaging apps) you have not yet opened. This permission lets the App read the content of notifications posted by other apps on your device.

  • Notification content read for this feature is processed and stored only on your device, inside the App's own local "Unseen" list.
  • It is not uploaded to our servers, not sent to any third party, and not used for advertising.
  • You grant this permission explicitly in Android Settings → Notification access, and can revoke it at any time — doing so disables only the Unseen feature.

3. On-Device Processing — What Never Leaves Your Device

The following categories of data are processed locally only and are never transmitted by the App to our servers or to any third party:

  • Your contacts (names, numbers) and any edits you make to them.
  • Your call log / call history.
  • Phone numbers you look up, and the spam/caller-ID result returned (matched against a bundled seed database and a local cache — no network request is made for a lookup).
  • Notification previews read for the "Unseen" feature.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected by the third-party SDKs above is used to show and measure advertising, understand how the App is used in aggregate so we can fix bugs and improve features, and diagnose crashes. We do not use your contacts, call log, notification content, or locally-matched caller-ID data for advertising.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal information. The only parties we share data with are the service providers listed in Section 4, each acting for the purposes described above. We do not share your contacts, call log, or notification content with anyone.

7. Important Safety Notice

Spam-call detection and call-screening are provided on a best-effort basis using a local database and may not identify every spam or fraudulent call, and may occasionally flag a legitimate call. Do not rely on this App for emergency communications. Always ensure you can reach emergency services using your device's standard calling method, regardless of this App's default-dialer or call-screening settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Spam Shield Pro is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Data Retention and Your Choices

  • There is no account to delete, because the App does not require one.
  • On-device data (cached contacts/call-log display data, the local "Unseen" list) is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.
  • Data held by the third-party SDKs in Section 4 is retained and deleted according to each provider's own policy. You may reset your Android Advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings at any time.
  • To ask about or request deletion of any data associated with your device identifiers, email thanhmichltd@gmail.com.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA). Contact us to exercise these rights; we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Security

11. International Data Transfers
